# Code of Conduct

> **Solo-maintainer adaptation of Contributor Covenant 2.1.** Sections 1–3 below are from the standard Covenant. Section 4 ("Enforcement") has been rewritten to match the project's actual maintenance posture — this is a solo-maintained project with no committee, no enforcement team, and no guaranteed response time. See [ADR-009 in `work-docs/PLAN-oss-readiness-audit.md`](work-docs/PLAN-oss-readiness-audit.md) for the rationale.

## 1. Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## 2. Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## 3. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces — GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and any official extension of those spaces (e.g., the project's PyPI page, plugin marketplace listings).

## 4. Enforcement (solo-maintainer adaptation)

This project is solo-maintained. There is no enforcement committee. **Before contributing**, please read the posture below in full so the expectation is set — if this enforcement model is unacceptable for your contribution, the project may not be the right fit, and you are welcome to fork it under MIT and run your own.

The following reflects what is actually offered:

- **Reporting channel.** Conduct concerns can be sent through the [SECURITY disclosure channel](SECURITY.md) (set "Conduct" in the subject) or by email to `e839638@gmail.com`. Both are read by one person.
- **Response time.** Best-effort. There is no committed timeline. Allow days, not hours.
- **Verification.** The maintainer will not act on a report he cannot independently verify with the evidence provided. Screenshots, links, and timestamps help. Hearsay does not.
- **Actions.** For violations the maintainer can act on, responses range from a private note to a block from the repository. Repeated or severe violations result in immediate blocking.
- **Appeals.** Sending a follow-up message explaining additional context. There is no formal appeal process.
