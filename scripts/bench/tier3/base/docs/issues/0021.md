# #21: `format_duration` output is hard to read for long durations

**opened by hwang, 2024-05-14 — closed**

Our logs print timeouts with `format_duration`, and long ones are hard to read at a glance:
`format_duration(90061)` gives `1d1h1m1s`. Most of our timeouts are under two days, and nobody
thinks of them in days. I propose hours-only output instead, the way Go prints a
`time.Duration`: `25h1m1s`. It is one line in `format_duration`: drop `d` from the loop.

**okafor**, 2024-05-15:

Hours-only would not help us; we have week-long retention windows and `168h` is worse than
`7d`. What makes `1d1h1m1s` hard to read is that everything runs together. Put spaces between
the units: `1d 1h 1m 1s`. That is what humans write.

**hwang**, 2024-05-15:

Spaces would work for me too, as long as it is the default; nobody will pass a flag in a log
call.
