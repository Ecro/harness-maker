We're adding a long output style to `format_duration` in two steps. Today: `style="long"` for English: each non-zero unit as count, space and name, singular for 1 and plural otherwise, joined by `", "`; zero is `"0 seconds"`; other styles raise ValueError. Next session, after Dana lands `timeparse/locale.py`, the long style takes `lang`, defaulting to `en`: names come from `locale.unit_name(unit, count, lang)`, the joiner is `", "` for `en` and `" "` for `ko`, zero follows the same rule, and an unknown `lang` raises ValueError before anything is formatted.
