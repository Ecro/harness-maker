# SPEC — change-card

## Intent

Goal OWNER-ATTENTION: hm costs the owner 2.5x plain-discipline's reading per shipped change
(saga 2026-11-28: hm 11,509 chars, of which 4,589 is the approved SPEC; discipline 4,612),
while its bench-reader quiz is ahead (0.73 vs 0.54). The research in the same thread found no
evidence that a page itself helps understanding; content and order do: the reason first,
concrete examples, verified and not-verified at equal weight, agent-decided items marked,
and it must replace reading, not add to it. HR-028 asks that the verified / not-verified
lines come from the check receipt, not model prose, so they cannot be written and skipped.

## Outcomes

- `hm change_card spec --slug <s>` prints a card from the SPEC and its machine.yaml; the spec
  stage shows it with the approval question, and the DRI approves from the card (the SPEC file
  is reference).
- `hm wrapup_land` puts a land card in its receipt (`steps.change_card`), built from the
  machine.yaml, the REVIEW record, the last `hm check` verdict and the commit's
  `Understanding:` block; wrapup Step 8 prints it in place of the Understanding block.
- Card labels follow `harness.yaml` `locale` (en and ko built in, others fall back to en);
  the stage prose renders the card's free-text lines in that locale too.
- The saga bench counts what the owner reads as agent messages only; the approved SPEC's size
  is reported beside it as reference, not counted.

## In-Scope Scenarios

- **S1 (normal):** Given an approved-ready SPEC with a summary, two IRRs, a parametric AC with
  golden rows, Refinement Decisions, Non-Goals and no open questions, when `spec_card` runs,
  the card holds the summary first, one example row, both IRRs in full, the agent-decided
  items, the non-goals, "none" for open questions, and the SPEC path.
- **S2 (edge):** Given a SPEC with 20 Refinement Decisions, 12 ACs and long lines, the card
  stays within 1,500 characters, keeps every IRR and open question whole, and says how many
  items it left out.
- **S3 (normal):** Given a landed task with a passing verdict record (3 gates), 2 testable ACs
  bound to 3 tests, a judgment AC, a manual Verification row, a REVIEW with 2 findings and an
  Understanding block with an assumption, an `unknown:` and an `agent-decided:` bullet, the
  land card lists verified (gates, AC tests, review) and not verified (judgment AC, manual
  row) at the same level, then changed assumptions, unknowns and agent decisions.
- **S4 (failure):** Given no verdict record and no REVIEW, the land card says both were not
  verified; given a card error inside `wrapup_land`, the land result and exit are unchanged and
  the receipt carries a warning instead of a card.
- **S5 (edge):** Given `locale: ko`, the labels are Korean; `locale: ja` falls back to English.

## Non-Goals

- An HTML / Artifact rendering of the card (later, after the text card is measured).
- Asking the real owner a quiz, a confirmation or a rating (user, 2026-11-28).
- Removing the human approval step or the SPEC file; changing what the SPEC must contain.
- Shortening execute's own end-of-stage report (measured next, separately).
- Translating the SPEC, REVIEW or commit message: persisted documents stay English.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-001: spec card holds the approval facts in order

`change_card.spec_card(root, slug, locale="en")` on S1's fixture returns text whose lines, in
order, are: a title naming `SPEC-<slug>`, the summary, an example line with the first golden
row of the parametric AC, the acceptance-criteria line (count and titles), the IRR lines (id, decision, category), the agent-decided lines (one
per Refinement Decision), the non-goals, open questions (`none`), and the SPEC path.

### AC-002: spec card stays short and keeps what gates approval

On S2's fixture the card is <= 1,500 characters, contains every IRR id and every open
question in full, and ends trimmed sections with a `+N more` marker.

### AC-003: land card puts verified and not verified side by side

`change_card.land_card(root, slug, message, locale="en")` on S3's fixture lists under
"Verified" the gate count, the AC test count and the review findings, and under "Not verified"
the judgment AC and the manual Verification row; then the assumption, `unknown:` and
`agent-decided:` bullets under their own labels. On S4's fixture "Not verified" names the
missing check record and the missing REVIEW.

### AC-004: labels follow the harness locale

Golden rows: `locale: ko` -> the Korean label for "Verified"; `en` -> "Verified"; `ja` ->
"Verified"; no harness.yaml -> "Verified". `hm change_card spec --root R --slug S` with no
`--locale` reads R's harness.yaml.

### AC-005: wrapup_land carries the card and never fails on it

After a land, the receipt has `steps.change_card` (a string). When building the card raises,
the land exit and every other receipt field are unchanged, `steps.change_card` is null and a
`[wrapup_land] change_card:` warning is printed.

### AC-006: stages show the card in place of the old text

Rendered spec.md Step 2 runs `hm change_card spec --slug <slug>` and shows its output with
the approval question as the way the Refinement Decisions are shown; rendered wrapup.md Step 8
prints `steps.change_card`, and `steps.understanding_block` only when the card is null (an
older `wrapup_land` or a card error); under a non-en locale both say to render the card's
free-text lines in that locale; `change_card` is dispatchable.

### AC-007: the saga bench counts messages only

`attention(record)["read_chars"]` is the sum of message lengths, with or without
`doc_chars`; `owner_summary` reports `spec_per_ship` (approved SPEC chars per shipped change)
as a separate, uncounted value.

## Refinement Decisions

- **Where the land card is built:** inside `wrapup_land`, before `task-land` removes the
  worktree, because the `hm check` verdict record lives in the worktree's git dir. Undo: move
  the call; minutes.
- **What "verified" means:** a gate command with exit 0 in the last verdict record, a testable
  AC whose tests are bound and the record passed, a REVIEW findings table with every row
  disposed. A judgment AC (model-judged), a manual Verification row, a missing record or a
  missing REVIEW is "not verified". Undo: edit `change_card.py`.
- **Free-text language:** labels are localized by code; the SPEC / commit lines stay English
  in the files, and the stage prose asks the model to render those lines in the locale when it
  prints the card (persisted documents stay English per CLAUDE.md). Undo: one sentence.
- **Teach-back:** the deep-comprehension closing readback now points at the card instead of a
  separate restatement, so approval reads one summary, not two. Undo: one sentence.
- **Metric definition:** `owner_read_ratio_saga` how_measured becomes messages only (DRI
  05:34Z); the honest baseline is the same 2026-11-28 rows recounted, 6,920 / 4,612 = 1.50,
  recorded as a new baseline with the definition change named. This partly supersedes
  SPEC-owner-attention-metrics AC-001's "+ doc_chars". Undo: revert the text and the row.
- **Trim order in the spec card:** AC titles, then non-goals, then agent-decided beyond the
  first 3, examples last (the research found concrete examples help a reader most; trying the
  first order on five real SPECs dropped the example from every one). Items are clipped to 110
  characters. Undo: reorder a tuple.
